About SRM & Our Cafeteria

About SRM Institute

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) is one of India's top-ranked higher education institutions, renowned for academic excellence, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and vibrant campus life. Serving thousands of students, faculty, and visitors daily across multiple campuses, SRM fosters an environment of innovation, diversity, and holistic development.

Our Cafeteria Experience

Located at the heart of the campus, SRM CAFETERIA serves as the ultimate hangout spot for students and staff. Established to provide healthy, hygienic, and affordable culinary options, we serve freshly brewed artisanal coffees, baked delights, and popular Indian street delicacies prepared under strict quality standards.

About This Website

This web portal is designed as a **pure HTML5 and CSS3 front-end project** for Computer Science coursework. It features a responsive layout, modern UI/UX design, interactive food ordering form with multiple payment modes, and a pure CSS modal pop-up system for seamless table booking confirmation without heavy external libraries.
